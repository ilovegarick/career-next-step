Run a 10-minute activity

Use this at the end of an advising appointment or in a workshop for people who have run through their usual applications and want somewhere else to start.

  1. Choose, 2 minutes. Let each person decide which option feels doable. If nobody comes to mind, prepare a question to rehearse independently or bring to a workshop.
  2. Practice, 5 minutes. Ask them to put one question in their own words. Offer quiet rehearsal, writing on paper, or practice with a partner. Sharing aloud is optional.
  3. Plan, 3 minutes. Have each person choose a specific action and time. Offer a private copy or printout. They can keep the draft and decide later whether to send it.

Adapt it to your setting

Before the session, identify one upcoming event and one way to reach an advisor. Use a familiar role or industry in your example. With paper, ask participants to write three lines: what I’ll say, what I’ll do, and when.

Check whether it helped

Ask participants whether they can name an action and when they plan to take it. At an optional one-week check-in, ask what they tried and what got in the way. Solo rehearsal counts when that’s the action the participant chose. Keep individual drafts private.
