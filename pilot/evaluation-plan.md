# One-workshop pilot

**Question**  
Can a job seeker who is hesitant to reach out leave with a specific action and a time to try it?

**Setup**  
Invite a workshop facilitator and an advising colleague to run the activity once. Offer the same exercise on paper. Ask staff to note common points of confusion without recording participants’ drafts or details.

**At the end**  
Ask “Do you have one action and a time you plan to try it?” Offer Yes, Partly, and Not yet. Record counts by response and total attendees. Ask staff how many minutes the exercise took and where people needed help.

**Optional one-week check-in**  
Through the center’s usual approved channel, ask “Did you try the step you chose?” and “What helped or got in the way?” Report the number of respondents alongside the number who tried a step. Use aggregate findings in any portfolio write-up with permission.

**Decision**  
If participants struggle to choose an action, revise the options and test again. If they make plans but struggle to act, review the reported barriers before changing the activity. If the activity fits the session and participants can use it independently, consider testing it in a different setting.

**Example pilot cases**  
Start with fictional scenarios covering a community member who knows nobody in their field and a student with a former instructor they could contact. This pilot measures a near-term behavior and does not establish employment impact.
